Concise
/kənˈsaɪs/
Meaning
Expressing something clearly using only a few words — short, but complete and to the point.
Example
"Please write a concise description of the bug so the team can understand it quickly."
Malayalam Hint
സംക്ഷിപ്തമായ (samkshipthamaaya) — വളരെ കൂടുതൽ വാക്കുകൾ ഉപയോഗിക്കാതെ കാര്യം പറയുക. "To the point" എന്ന് ഇംഗ്ലീഷിൽ പറയുന്ന അർഥം.
Tip
Use concise to praise good writing or speech: "Your email was concise and clear." Developers often say "keep it concise" in code reviews and documentation. Opposite: verbose (too wordy).
