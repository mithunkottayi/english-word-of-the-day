Grateful
/ ˈɡreɪt.fəl /
Meaning
Feeling or showing thanks because someone has done something kind or helpful for you.
Example "I am truly grateful for your help in fixing the bug before the deadline."
Malayalam Hint നന്ദിയുള്ള (nandiyulla) — ആരെങ്കിലും നല്ലത് ചെയ്തതിന് നന്ദി തോന്നുക. "Thankful" പോലെ, പക്ഷേ കൂടുതൽ ഹൃദ്യവും formal-ഉം ആണ്.
Usage Tip Use grateful in professional emails and messages — it sounds more sincere than simply saying "thanks". Try: "I'm grateful for your support" or "We are grateful for this opportunity."
