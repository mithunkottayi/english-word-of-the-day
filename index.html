Word of the Day
concise
/kənˈsaɪs/
Meaning
Expressing ideas clearly and fully using as few words as possible.
Example
"His concise commit message helped the whole team understand the fix right away."
Malayalam Hint
ചുരുക്കമായ (churukkamaya) — ആവശ്യമുള്ള കാര്യം മാത്രം, കൂടുതൽ വാക്കുകൾ ഇല്ലാതെ.
Short and to the point — no extra words.
Usage Tip
Use concise to praise someone's clear, brief writing or speech. Try: "Please keep your pull request description concise" or "She gave a concise summary of the bug."
