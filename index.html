Word of the Day
persist
/pərˈsɪst/
Meaning
To keep doing something firmly and steadily, even when it is hard or others want you to stop.
Example "He persisted with the bug fix until the application worked perfectly."
Malayalam Hint  🇮🇳 തുടർന്നു ശ്രമിക്കുക — keep trying without giving up, no matter what.
Usage Tip Pair it with in or with: "persist in your efforts" or "persist with a difficult task." Never say "persist on."
