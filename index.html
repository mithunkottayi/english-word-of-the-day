Diligent
/ˈdɪl.ɪ.dʒənt/
adjective
Meaning

Working hard and carefully with steady, consistent effort over time — not rushing, but always giving your best.

Example A diligent developer always reviews their code before pushing it, catching small bugs before they become big problems.
Malayalam Hint അധ്വാനശീലൻ / ഉത്സാഹമുള്ള ബുദ്ധിമുട്ടുണ്ടായാലും ശ്രദ്ധയോടെ, ക്ഷമയോടെ ജോലി ചെയ്യുന്ന ആൾ — ആ ഗുണമാണ് "diligent."
Usage Tip "Diligent" shines in professional contexts — resumes, performance reviews, client emails. Try: "She is a diligent team member who never misses a deadline." It's a strong, positive adjective that any manager loves to hear.
