Clarify
/ ˈklær.ɪ.faɪ /
Meaning
To make something easier to understand by explaining it more clearly, or to remove confusion about it.
💬 Example "Could you please clarify the requirements before I start building the Laravel API?"
🌴 Malayalam Hint വ്യക്തമാക്കുക (vyakthamakkuka) — to make something clear or free from doubt.
💡 Usage Tip Use "clarify" in meetings, emails, and code reviews when something is unclear. "Can you clarify this ticket?" sounds much more professional than "What do you mean?"
