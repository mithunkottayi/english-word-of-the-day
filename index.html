Word of the Day
Persist
/pərˈsɪst/
Meaning
To continue doing something firmly despite difficulty, opposition, or failure — to keep going without giving up.
Example

"He persisted with his English practice every morning, and within three months his confidence had completely changed."

Malayalam Hint

തുടർച്ചയായി ശ്രമിക്കുക — like when you refuse to quit something even when it gets hard. In Laravel too, you persist data to a database — same root idea: making something continue to exist.

Usage Tip

Use persist in professional settings: "I'll persist with this bug fix." Also common: persistence (noun) — "Success comes from persistence, not perfection."
