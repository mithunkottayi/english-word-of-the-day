Clarify
/ˈklær.ɪ.faɪ/
Meaning
To make something easier to understand by explaining it more clearly, or to remove confusion about it.
Example "Could you clarify what you mean by 'urgent'? I want to make sure I finish the right task first."
Malayalam Hint വ്യക്തമാക്കുക (vyakthamaakkuka) Something unclear becomes clear — just like debugging code until the error makes sense!
Usage Tip Use clarify in meetings, client emails, or Slack when something is confusing — it sounds polite and professional. Try: "Let me clarify…" or "Can you clarify that requirement?"
