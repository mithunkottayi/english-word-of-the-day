Articulate
/ ɑːr-TIK-yoo-lit /
Meaning
Able to express ideas and thoughts clearly and effectively in speech or writing.
Example
"She was so articulate during the client meeting that everyone immediately understood her project plan."
Malayalam Hint (മലയാളം)
വ്യക്തമായി സംസാരിക്കാൻ കഴിയുന്ന — vyaktamāyi samsārikān kazhiyunna.
ചിന്തകൾ സ്പഷ്ടമായി പ്രകടിപ്പിക്കാൻ ശേഷിയുള്ളത്.
Usage Tip
Use it as an adjective to compliment someone: "He is very articulate."
Also a verb: "Can you articulate your idea more clearly?" — useful in code reviews and standups!
