✨ Word of the Day
Concise
/kənˈsaɪs/
adjective
📖 Meaning
Expressing much in few words — short, clear, and to the point, without unnecessary details.
💬 Example
"His bug report was concise — just two sentences, but it explained everything the team needed to fix the issue."
🌿 Malayalam Hint
ചുരുക്കമായി പറഞ്ഞ (churukkamaayi paranja) Think of how we say "ചുരുക്കി പറ" ("say it briefly") — concise is that quality in English. When your manager says "keep it short", they want you to be concise.
💡 Usage Tip
Use concise to praise good writing or speaking. You can say:
• "Please write a concise summary."
• "That was a very concise explanation!"
In professional emails, being concise is a superpower. Less is more!
