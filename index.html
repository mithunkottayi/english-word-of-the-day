Clarify
/ˈklærɪfaɪ/
Meaning
To make something easier to understand by explaining it more clearly or giving more details.
Example "Could you clarify what you meant in your last message? I want to make sure I fix the right issue."
Malayalam Hint വ്യക്തമാക്കുക (Vyaktamakkuka) — to make something clear or plain.
When you ask a client "clarify cheyyamo?" you're asking them to explain better.
Usage Tip Use clarify in meetings or chats instead of "explain more." It sounds more professional. Try: "Can you clarify the requirements?" or "Let me clarify — I meant the staging server, not production."
