✦ Word of the Day ✦
Concise
/kənˈsaɪs/  ·  kun-SISE
adjective
📖 Meaning
Expressing a lot of information clearly and in only a few words — short but complete.
💬 Example
"His error message was concise — just one clear sentence that told the user exactly what went wrong."
🌿 Malayalam Hint
ചുരുക്കമായി (churukkamaayi) — ആവശ്യമുള്ളത് മാത്രം, അനാവശ്യ വാക്കുകൾ ഇല്ലാതെ.
"Be concise" = ചുരുക്കി പറയൂ
💡 Usage Tip
In professional emails and code comments, concise writing shows respect for the reader's time. Instead of writing three sentences, try saying the same thing in one — and say it clearly.

Try it: "Could you give me a concise summary of the bug?"
