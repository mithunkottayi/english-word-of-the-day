Word of the Day
clarify
/ˈklærɪfaɪ/
verb
Meaning
To make something easier to understand by explaining it more clearly; to remove confusion or doubt about something.
Example
"Could you clarify what you mean by 'deploy by Friday'? I want to make sure I deliver exactly what you need."
Malayalam Hint
വ്യക്തമാക്കുക (vyakthamaakkuka) — "clarify" എന്നാൽ ഒരു കാര്യം കൂടുതൽ വ്യക്തമായി വിശദീകരിക്കുക എന്നാണ്. Doubt ഉള്ളപ്പോൾ ചോദിക്കുക: "Can you clarify?"
Tip
Use "clarify" in professional emails when requirements are unclear — it sounds polite and shows attention to detail. Try: "Just to clarify, should this feature work for all users or only admins?"
