Word of the Day
persist
/pərˈsɪst/
Meaning
To keep doing something firmly and steadily, especially when it is difficult or when others oppose you.
Example
"He persisted in learning new English words every day, and his confidence grew quickly."
Malayalam Hint
തുടരുക (Thudaruka) — ബുദ്ധിമുട്ടുകൾ ഉണ്ടായാലും ഒരു കാര്യം വിടാതെ തുടർന്ന് ചെയ്യുക. "Persist" ചെയ്യുന്ന ആൾ ഒരിക്കലും ഉപേക്ഷിക്കില്ല.
Usage Tip
Use "persist in doing" or "persist with" something.
✦ "She persisted with her studies despite the long hours."
✦ "Don't give up — persist and you will succeed!"
