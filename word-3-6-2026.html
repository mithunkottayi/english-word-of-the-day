persist
/pərˈsɪst/
Meaning
To continue doing something despite difficulties or opposition; to keep going without giving up.
Example
"He persisted with his English practice every day, and within months his confidence had grown remarkably."
Malayalam Hint
തുടർന്ന് ശ്രമിക്കുക (thudarnju shramikkuka) — തടസ്സങ്ങൾ ഉണ്ടെങ്കിലും ശ്രമം നിർത്താതെ തുടരുക.
Keep trying without stopping, even when things are hard.
Usage Tip
Use "persist" to describe someone who doesn't quit under pressure.
"Persist in your efforts" is formal; in casual speech say "keep at it" or "don't give up."
Bonus for developers: In Laravel, data persistence means saving data to the database permanently!
