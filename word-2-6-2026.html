Clarify
/ˈklær.ɪ.faɪ/
Meaning
To make something easier to understand by explaining it more clearly, or to remove confusion about it.
Example "Could you clarify what you mean by that feature? I want to make sure I build it correctly."
Malayalam Hint വ്യക്തമാക്കുക  (Vyakthamakkuka) — ഒരു കാര്യം ആർക്കെങ്കിലും കൂടുതൽ വ്യക്തമായി പറഞ്ഞ് മനസ്സിലാക്കിക്കുക.
Usage Tip Use "clarify" in client emails or team meetings when something is unclear. Say "Let me clarify…" instead of "Let me explain again" — it sounds more professional and respectful.
