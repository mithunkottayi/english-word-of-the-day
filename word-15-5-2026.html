Word of the Day
convenient
/kən-VEEN-yənt/
Meaning
Easy to use, access, or do; saves time and effort; fits well into your situation.
Example
"It is very convenient to use Laravel's built-in authentication system instead of writing everything from scratch."
Malayalam Hint
സൗകര്യപ്രദം (saukaryapradham) — something that is easy and handy to use or access.
Tip
Use "convenient" when something makes life easier or fits your schedule. A polite phrase to remember: "Is this time convenient for you?" — perfect for scheduling meetings professionally.
