Word of the Day
persist
/pərˈsɪst/
Meaning
To keep doing something even when it is hard or takes a long time; to not give up.
Example
"Mithun persisted in practising English every evening, and within a few months he could speak with real confidence."
Malayalam Hint
ഉറച്ചു നിൽക്കുക (urachhu nilkkuka) — ബുദ്ധിമുട്ടുണ്ടെങ്കിലും തുടർന്നു ശ്രമിക്കുക.
Bonus: Laravel-ൽ നിങ്ങൾ ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്ന "data persistence" എന്നതും ഇതേ root word-ൽ നിന്നാണ്!
Tip
Use persist in + verb-ing, or persist with + noun.
✔ "She persisted in asking questions."
✔ "He persisted with his studies despite the challenges."
The adjective form persistent is great for resumes: "a persistent problem-solver."
