Word of the Day
Elaborate
/ɪˈlæb.ər.ət/
Meaning
To explain something in more detail; or something that is very detailed and carefully planned.
Example
"Could you elaborate on how the payment gateway is integrated in your Laravel project?"
Malayalam Hint
വിശദീകരിക്കുക (vishadeekarikkuka) — to explain in detail.
"Elaborate" is used when you want someone to say more about a topic, just like asking someone to vishadeekarikkuka.
Usage Tip
Pro tip: In meetings or emails, instead of "Can you say more?", say "Could you elaborate on that?" — it sounds confident and professional. As a developer, you can also say: "Let me elaborate on the architecture."
