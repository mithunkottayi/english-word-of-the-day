Word of the Day
Persistent
/pərˈsɪstənt/
Meaning
Continuing firmly and without giving up, even when things are difficult. Also used in technology to mean data or a connection that lasts over time.
Example
"Mithun was persistent in tracking down the Laravel bug — he spent two hours debugging and finally fixed it."
Malayalam Hint
നിരന്തരം ശ്രമിക്കുന്ന (nirantaram shramikkunna) — ഒരു കാര്യം ഉപേക്ഷിക്കാതെ തുടർന്ന് ചെയ്യുന്ന; never giving up on a task. In tech: persistent storage = ഡേറ്റ നഷ്ടമാകാതെ സൂക്ഷിക്കൽ.
Tip
Use "persistent" both professionally and in daily life. Say: "She is a persistent learner" or "This session uses a persistent database connection." It shows strength and dedication — a great word to use about yourself!
